Animals, outfits and treasures

Page Pet has ten animals to choose from. Eight are free; the red panda and lamb come with the optional Goodies pack. Switch animals any time from the popup. Your pet keeps its name, stats and collection.

The ten animals

Cat

An orange tabby with stripes, whiskers and a long tail that curls when it is happy. The pet most people start with. It says meow.

Dog

A floppy-eared pup with a patch of darker fur. Its tail wags hardest when you throw it the ball. It says woof!.

Rabbit

Long ears and a cotton tail. It thumps a foot to say hello. It says *thump*.

Fox

A russet fox with a big bushy tail, quick on its feet. It says yip!.

Bear

A round brown bear cub, slow and cuddly. It says grr.

Panda

Black-and-white and always hungry. Great company on long reading sessions. It says *munch*.

Pig

A pink piglet with a curly tail and a big snout for digging up treasures. It says oink.

Frog

A green frog that hops along the page instead of walking. It says ribbit.

Red panda Goodies pack

A ginger red panda with a fluffy tail. Part of the Goodies pack, and you can try it on your own pet for a minute first. It says squeak!.

Lamb Goodies pack

A fluffy white lamb. Part of the Goodies pack, with a free one-minute try-on. It says baa.

Outfits

The Goodies pack adds four outfits: a bow, a scarf, a flower crown and round glasses. Every animal can wear every outfit. Tap a locked outfit in the popup to see it on your own pet for a minute before deciding.

The ten treasures

Your pet keeps a collection book. Four treasures turn up when it digs; six are earned by what you do, at most one of each per day.
