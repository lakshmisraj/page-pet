Page Pet for Microsoft Edge

Page Pet is a free virtual pet extension for Microsoft Edge on Windows, Mac and Linux. A small cat, dog, fox or panda walks along the bottom of the pages you visit, and you look after it from the toolbar.

Get it for Edge free

Install it from Edge Add-ons

  1. Open Page Pet on the Microsoft Edge Add-ons site and click Get.
  2. Edge lists what the extension can do. Click Add extension.
  3. Reload tabs that were already open. The pet appears on every page you open or reload from then on.

Edge can also install from the Chrome Web Store: turn on Allow extensions from other stores when Edge offers it. Both copies are the same extension, so pick one.

Show it in the toolbar

Click the Extensions (puzzle piece) button beside the address bar, then the eye icon next to Page Pet so it shows in the toolbar. Its cat-face button opens the popup: feed your pet, throw it a ball, choose an animal and see the treasures it has collected.

Sleeping tabs and efficiency mode

Edge puts background tabs to sleep to save memory. Your pet pauses with the tab and carries on when you come back. Its tummy, joy and energy are saved in the extension, not in the tab, so nothing is lost.

The pet isn't showing up

  • Reload the tab. Tabs opened before installing don't have the pet yet.
  • Some pages can't have it: edge:// pages, the new tab page, the Edge Add-ons store and the PDF viewer don't allow extensions to draw on them.
  • It might be hidden: open the popup and check Show on pages, Snooze 1 hour and Hide on this site.
  • InPrivate windows: extensions are off there unless you tick Allow in InPrivate under edge://extensions.

Is it safe?

Edge says the extension can "read and change data on websites" because the pet is drawn on every page. Page Pet never reads what pages say or what you type, ignores password and payment fields, and keeps everything on your computer. Read the privacy policy.
