Page Pet help

Everything is done from the popup: click the Page Pet button in your browser's toolbar. If it isn't there, open the Extensions (puzzle piece) menu and pin it.

Settings

How do I hide the pet on one website?

Open that website, click the Page Pet button in the toolbar, then Settings › Hide on this site. The pet stays away from that site on every visit until you turn it back on from the same place.

How do I pause the pet for a while?

Open the popup and choose Settings › Snooze 1 hour. Your pet naps until then and comes back by itself. Click Wake up to bring it back sooner.

How do I turn the pet off completely?

Untick Show on pages in the popup. The pet leaves every page but keeps its name, stats and collection, so you can turn it back on later.

How do I stop the pet talking?

Under Settings › Chattiness choose Quiet, so it only answers when you pet, feed or play with it, or Silent, so it never talks and only shows hearts and faces.

Looking after your pet

How do I feed my pet or play with it?

Open the popup and click Drop a cookie or Throw a ball. The treat falls onto the page and your pet runs to it. You can also click the pet to pat it, and drag it to pick it up.

How do I rename my pet?

Click its name at the top of the popup, type a new one and press Enter.

What do tummy, joy and energy mean?

Tummy goes down over time and up with treats. Joy goes up when you pat it and play. Energy drops as it runs about and comes back when it naps. While you are away they drain slowly, never for more than six hours’ worth and never below 15 percent.

Will the pet block buttons or links?

No. It walks off buttons, links and text boxes by itself, and when there is nowhere clear to stand it fades so your clicks go straight through to the page.

Your pet and your browsers

Does my pet move to my other computer?

Not yet. Your pet is saved only in this browser on this computer, so a new browser starts with a new pet. A Goodies pack purchase can be restored on another browser with Already paid? in the popup.

How do I restore a Goodies pack I already bought?

Open the popup, go to Goodies and click Already paid?. Sign in with the email you used to pay and the pack unlocks again.

Why is the pet not on this page?

Reload the tab first. Browser pages such as settings, the new tab page, extension stores and the built-in PDF viewer never allow extensions to draw on them. Also check Show on pages, Snooze and Hide on this site in the popup.

How do I uninstall Page Pet?

Right-click the Page Pet toolbar button and choose Remove from Chrome, Remove from Microsoft Edge or Remove Extension in Firefox. Everything it saved is deleted with it.
