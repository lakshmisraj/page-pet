Questions

Is Page Pet free?

Yes. All eight animals, the cookie and ball, treasures, and every setting are free. An optional one-time Goodies pack (US$2.99) adds two animals, four outfits, and special treats and toys.

Which browsers does Page Pet work in?

Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Arc use the Chrome version. Firefox 140 and later has its own version. A Safari version for Mac is in preparation.

Does Page Pet read what I type or the pages I visit?

No. It notices that a key was pressed so the pet can sit beside the text box, but never which key. It ignores password, payment-card and one-time-code fields completely, and nothing about your browsing leaves your computer.

Will the pet get in the way of buttons on a page?

It walks off buttons, links and text boxes by itself, and fades so clicks pass through when there is nowhere clear to stand. You can also make it quiet, snooze it for an hour, or hide it on any site.

What happens to my pet when I am away?

Its tummy and joy drain slowly, but never for more than six hours' worth and never below 15 percent. When you come back after a few hours it greets you with a present.
